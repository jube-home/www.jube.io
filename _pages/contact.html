Ask for a demo

The easiest way in is a short, no-obligation walkthrough of Jube against a scenario close to your own, before anything is signed. Say "demo" on WhatsApp or email to set one up.

What is on offer

  • The platform itself. Jube is open source (AGPLv3) and free to use. What you pay for, if anything, is expertise, not a licence.
  • Training and implementation. A fixed fee of USD 6,250: a 3-week curriculum, then a project phase to first pre-production traffic, delivered remotely.
  • Ongoing support. Flat monthly tiers from USD 350 (Sandbox) to USD 2,400 (Enterprise, with 24/7 callout), sized to your deployment rather than to a block of hours.

Requesting a quote

There is no form. Message the developer with the details a quote needs:

  • the full legal name of the organisation that will deploy Jube
  • for training, the people who will attend and their roles, your target go-live, your working week and time zone
  • for support, your environment, regions and monthly transaction volume
  • whether you are buying directly or through an intermediary

The quote carries only those engagement-specific details. Fees and inclusions are already published on the training and support pages.
