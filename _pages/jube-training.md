## How it runs

**Curriculum, weeks 1 to 3.** Three sessions a week, each up to 3 hours, with a practical
own-time exercise after each phase. By the end, your team has configured rules,
workflows and models hands-on, and has built and chaos-tested a high-availability
cluster.

**Project phase, from week 4.** You build the deployment against your own environment
and data, with implementation review meetings twice a week and asynchronous support over
WhatsApp, Jira and Loom, until the first pre-production traffic flows. That typically
happens within about 12 weeks of the first session.

In private training, you will gain:

- Hands-on configuration of rules, workflows and machine learning models, in scenarios
  drawn from your own data
- A pre-production deployment, including a chaos-tested high-availability cluster
  (Docker Swarm, Patroni, Redis Sentinel, HAProxy)
- Direct access to the developer throughout, for architectural correctness and
  certification
- Delivery built around your team's schedule, experience and objectives, over Google
  Meet, with every session recorded

The Jube developer delivers every session, so the knowledge is first-hand from
compliance fundamentals through to production deployment and C# extensibility.

built around that as a matter of course:

- The training week follows your working week (for example Sunday to Thursday), not a

- Ramadan is planned for, with shortened days or a schedule that avoids it entirely,
  as you prefer.

  the day.

## Training plan

11 sessions, each followed by a practical own-time exercise, building toward a complete
pre-production implementation. Sessions 2 to 7 and 9 to 11 are the 9 core sessions,
delivered 3 a week of up to 3 hours across weeks 1 to 3. Session 1 checks your
prerequisites before week 1, and Session 8 is an elective scheduled to suit your team.

- Session 1 (1 hour): Prerequisites and dress rehearsal
- Session 2 (3 hours): From regulation to real-time
- Session 3 (3 hours): From data to decisions
- Session 4 (3 hours): Precision, memory and intelligence
- Session 5 (3 hours): Managing the human side
- Session 6 (3 hours): Access, data and insight
- Session 7 (3 hours): Extending the platform
- Session 8 (2 hours, elective): Transaction flow analysis and Redis cache internals
- Session 9 (3 hours): Production-grade deployment
- Session 10 (3 hours): C# architecture patterns and solution structure
- Session 11 (3 hours): C# background threads and transaction processing

Practical exercises run throughout: a transaction monitoring strategy, a
compliance-grade case management system, chaos engineering a live cluster, and an
analytical dashboard.

## Practicalities

**Integration support** continues async-first through WhatsApp, Jira and Loom, on the
same ticket-and-Loom-video model used in ongoing [support](/jube-support/). This period
is the natural introduction to that model, and it sizes any follow-on tier from evidence
rather than a guess. Implementation review meetings run twice a week through the
project phase.

**Training servers** are dedicated cloud instances (DigitalOcean, Frankfurt or
Singapore), live for 2 months in isolated tenants. Participants need Git, Docker and a
running Jube instance beforehand, validated in Session 1.

**Custom gap development** (2 included days) closes any gap between your environment
and the platform, such as a Kafka integration in place of RabbitMQ or HTTP. It is
identified and delivered during the lead time, and it is built into the core product under
AGPLv3, not held as a private fork.

**Scheduling.** Jube starts one training and implementation client a month, with a
typical 2-month lead time from signature to the first session. This is a capacity
constraint, not a sales device, because delivery is personal to the developer.

**After go-live.** Following successful implementation and first live transactions,
ongoing [support](/jube-support/) is available directly from the Jube developer.

**Ownership and terms.** Your Inline Scripts, custom DLLs and configuration remain your
property, as set out in the [AGPLv3 compliance guide](/jube-agplv3-open-source-compliance/).
Insurance, liability and the contracting entity are in the
[JTOS](/agreements/jtos-version-1-8), and evidence of current insurance is available on
request.
