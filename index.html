    Cornerstone · 01
    Validation, all the way down.

    Joobriks work through Jube's own service layer, with every check the platform applies to itself. If a claim can't be validated, you're told.

    Cornerstone · 02
    A Joobrik proposes. You decide.

    Approval and snapshotting came long before the agents. Joobriks inherit both, so nothing they do sits outside the record.

    Cornerstone · 03
    Every conversation, reconstructible.

    Every tool call, LLM call, query and routing decision, and what it cost, is kept against the conversation. If something needs explaining, it already can be. Live signals and an AI walkthrough of the flow then show where agent boundaries need tuning.

    Cornerstone · 04
    Only what you're allowed to touch.

    Your permissions are checked before a Joobrik is offered any tool. The platform decides what it sees, not the LLM. If a Joobrik gets carried away and writes its own SQL, it is rewritten to enforce isolation, by tenant and by function. Nothing it runs in R can leave the sandbox.

    If Jube knows it, it's in the Pasture.

Agentic AI without a rich Pasture and real tools simply doesn't work. So it comes back to the same thing: "Just ask Jooby."

    Point it at commodity open-weights AI models. That's it.

    For years the juice wasn't worth the squeeze. DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash changed that: sparse expert routing and a compressed context cache make long agentic loops affordable, and bearable to wait on.

    Jooby needs three AI models Jube doesn't ship: an LLM, an embedding model and a reranker. The choice is made for you: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, BGE-M3 and BAAI BGE Reranker v2 M3. It's tested, tuned and supported as a set, and swapping models is not something we encourage (but you can).

    All three are open weights. No black-box API sits between your transactions and your answers, no vendor can retire or reprice the model underneath you, and you can run every one inside your own walls. Serve them from any provider that hosts all three, or from several commodity endpoints. Sovereignty demands it? Point the same three at your own endpoints, wherever suits you:

    02 · No more rule guesswork
