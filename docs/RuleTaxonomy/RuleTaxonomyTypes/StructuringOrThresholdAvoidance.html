    • Structuring or Threshold Avoidance

    Structuring or Threshold Avoidance

    Definition: values engineered to dodge a known limit, repeated.

    Construction: Double threshold extensions (IsJustBelowThreshold, IsRoundAmount) combined with Velocity and Volume or Cardinality for the repetition. Use it when the risk is evasion of a specific numeric control, and not a generic anomaly, which is Behavioral Baseline and Ratio. See the Rule Taxonomy.

    Question an analyst asks Rule Taxonomy type that answers it
    Are deposits repeatedly just under the 10,000 reporting limit? Structuring: IsJustBelowThreshold with a TTL Counter for the repetition
    Is an account always sending round amounts? Structuring: IsRoundAmount with a TTL Counter for the repetition
    Are amounts unusually large for this customer? Not this type: Behavioral Baseline and Ratio
    Is a single amount just under the limit, once? Structuring without repetition: a weak signal on its own, see below

    Structuring: The Single Event Signal is Weak

    A single amount just below a limit is weak evidence: most such amounts are legitimate. The signal is the repetition, so the rule has two parts. The first is a payload predicate that recognises an engineered value. The second is a counter of how often that has happened for the entity within a window. The two parts are separate Activation Rules, joined by a TTL Counter, following the shape of Velocity and Volume.

    Structuring: Construction Steps

    Step 1: Recognise the Engineered Value

    IsJustBelowThreshold(threshold, marginPercent) is true when the amount is below the threshold and within the margin percentage of it (IsJustAboveThreshold is the mirror, for a limit that triggers on exceeding it). For a 10,000 limit with a 10 percent margin, amounts from 9,000 up to but excluding 10,000 are recognised:

    Matched = Payload.CurrencyAmount.MatchIsJustBelowThreshold(10000, 10)

    IsRoundAmount(nearest) is true when the amount is an exact multiple of the value given, for example 500. Round amounts are a separate signal, and are often combined with a threshold band, using Require as the guard. See the Rule Pipeline Reference:

    Matched = Payload.CurrencyAmount.RequireIsRoundAmount(500).MatchInRange(5000, 9999)

    Where several limits apply, for example 3,000, 10,000 and 15,000, IsJustBelowAnyThreshold(marginPercent, thresholds...) tests all of them in one step, and takes the margin first:

    Matched = Payload.CurrencyAmount.MatchIsJustBelowAnyThreshold(10, 3000, 10000, 15000)

    The threshold, margin and rounding unit must come from the data and the control in question, per the Rule Taxonomy, and not from a list of best practice values.

    Step 2: Count the Repetition with a TTL Counter

    Define a TTL Counter keyed on the entity, windowed to the period the control is measured over (for example Days / 7), and increment it from the Activation Rule of Step 1. The Activation Rule that recognises the engineered value is the incrementing rule. Priority follows the rules in Velocity and Volume: incrementing earlier in the Priority order means the current event is included in the count.

    Step 3: Alert on the Repetition

    Matched = TTLCounter.JustBelowLimit7d.MatchGreaterOrEqual(3)

    Where the repetition has to be across different values or destinations, and not merely a count, use an Abstraction Rule with Distinct Count, per Cardinality, for example several just below limit amounts split across many beneficiaries. Where several payments sum past the limit inside a window, use a Sum TTL Counter, and threshold it against the limit directly:

    Matched = TTLCounter.DepositValue24h.RequireGreater(10000).Against(TTLCounter.DepositCount24h).RequireGreaterOrEqual(3).Against(Payload.CurrencyAmount).MatchIsJustBelowThreshold(10000, 10)

    Structuring: Common Faults

    Symptoms of a misbuilt Structuring rule, with the likely cause.

    Symptom in a Structuring rule Likely cause
    Too many alerts on single events No repetition condition. A single amount below a limit is weak evidence on its own.
    Structured amounts just over the margin missed The margin is too narrow for how the limit is being avoided. Derive the margin from the data.
    The count includes the alerting event or not Priority of the incrementing rule relative to the alerting rule. See Velocity and Volume.
    The limit is a sum across accounts The counter key is the account, not the beneficial owner. Key the counter on the entity the control applies to.

    Examples by Sub-Class

    Validated. Every fragment in this section is compiled individually through the Jube Parser, with its payload fields and its TTL Counter, Abstraction, Calculation, List and Sanction tokens declared, so each extension method and argument type is checked against the engine. Compilation does not validate a rule’s logic or its thresholds, which are illustrative placeholders to be tuned on data.

    9.1 Cash structuring (smurfing)

    # Example Description Jube construction Fragment Notes
    9.1-01 Cash deposits just below reporting threshold Deposits in a band just beneath the currency transaction report limit (e.g. 9,000–9,999 where the limit is 10,000). TTL counter CashDepositBand7d (Data Name AccountId, Interval Days / 7, count only) is incremented by the band Activation Rule at an earlier Priority than the alerting rule, which reads TTLCounter.CashDepositBand7d.MatchGreaterOrEqual(2), so the read includes this deposit. The limit, margin and TxnType value are placeholders. Matched = Payload.TxnType.RequireEqual("CashDeposit").Against(Payload.CurrencyAmount).MatchIsJustBelowThreshold(10000, 10) Check: Limit 10,000 is the US currency transaction report threshold (31 CFR 1010.311); other jurisdictions differ. Verify the limit that applies. Tag: Structuring · AML · Account · 7d, count ≥ 2 in band
    9.1-02 Multiple cash deposits aggregating above threshold within a day Several sub-threshold deposits whose same-day total exceeds the limit. TTL sum counter CashBandSum24h (Data Name CustomerId, Interval Hours / 24, Sum on with Data Value CurrencyAmount) is incremented by the band Activation Rule at an earlier Priority, and the alerting rule reads TTLCounter.CashBandSum24h.MatchGreater(10000), so the sum already includes this deposit. The window is rolling, not a business day, and CustomerId is a placeholder key the payload must carry. Matched = Payload.TxnType.RequireEqual("CashDeposit").Against(Payload.CurrencyAmount).MatchIsJustBelowThreshold(10000, 10) Check: Aggregation for the reporting threshold is by business day (US 31 CFR 1010.313), not a rolling 24 hours. Verify. Tag: Structuring · AML · Customer · 24h
    9.1-03 Multi-day cash deposits aggregating above threshold Rolling sum across consecutive days. TTL sum counter CashDepositSum5d (Data Name CustomerId, Interval Days / 5, Sum on with Data Value CurrencyAmount) is incremented by an Activation Rule that matches every cash deposit, and the alerting rule reads TTLCounter.CashDepositSum5d.MatchGreater(10000). This is a rolling five-day sum with no band and no consecutive-day test; add the band predicate from 9.1-01 to the incrementing rule to sum sub-threshold deposits only. Matched = TTLCounter.CashDepositSum5d.MatchGreater(10000) Tag: Structuring · AML · Customer · 5d
    9.1-04 Cash deposits split across branches Same-day deposits across multiple branches. Abstraction Rule CashDepositBranches24h (Search Key CustomerId, Search Interval Hours / 24, Function Type Distinct Count on BranchId) over the cash deposits its own Matched fragment selects, and the alerting rule reads Abstraction.CashDepositBranches24h.MatchGreaterOrEqual(2). No amount band is applied here; add the band predicate to the Abstraction’s Matched fragment to narrow it. Confirm whether the current event is in the cached set before fixing the count. Matched = Abstraction.CashDepositBranches24h.MatchGreaterOrEqual(2) Tag: Structuring · AML · Customer · 24h
    9.1-05 Cash deposits split across accounts Deposits into several accounts of one person. Abstraction Rule CashDepositAccounts24h (Search Key CustomerId, Search Interval Hours / 24, Function Type Distinct Count on AccountId) over cash deposits, read by Abstraction.CashDepositAccounts24h.MatchGreaterOrEqual(2). The Search Key must be the person, not the account, or the rule cannot see the person’s other accounts. Matched = Abstraction.CashDepositAccounts24h.MatchGreaterOrEqual(2) Tag: Structuring · AML · Customer · 24h
    9.1-06 Cash deposits by multiple depositors into one account Many individuals deposit just below the limit (smurfs). Abstraction Rule CashBandDepositors7d (Search Key AccountId, Search Interval Days / 7, Function Type Distinct Count on DepositorId, a placeholder field) over the band deposits of 9.1-01, read by Abstraction.CashBandDepositors7d.MatchGreaterOrEqual(3), where the count is a placeholder. Matched = Payload.TxnType.RequireEqual("CashDeposit").Against(Payload.CurrencyAmount).MatchIsJustBelowThreshold(10000, 10) Tag: Structuring · AML · Account · 7d
    9.1-07 Cash deposits split across ATMs Deposits across several deposit ATMs. Abstraction Rule CashDepositAtms24h (Search Key CustomerId, Search Interval Hours / 24, Function Type Distinct Count on AtmId) over cash deposits made through the ATM channel, read by Abstraction.CashDepositAtms24h.MatchGreaterOrEqual(2). Channel and AtmId are placeholder field names, and a band predicate can be added to narrow the match. Matched = Payload.TxnType.RequireEqual("CashDeposit").Against(Payload.Channel).MatchEqual("ATM") Tag: Structuring · AML · Customer · 24h
    9.1-08 Cash withdrawals just below reporting limit Withdrawals in a band under the limit. TTL counter CashWithdrawBand7d (Data Name AccountId, Interval Days / 7, count only) is incremented by the band Activation Rule at an earlier Priority, and the alerting rule reads TTLCounter.CashWithdrawBand7d.MatchGreaterOrEqual(2). The count of 2 is carried over by analogy with the deposit rule and is not a source figure. Matched = Payload.TxnType.RequireEqual("CashWithdrawal").Against(Payload.CurrencyAmount).MatchIsJustBelowThreshold(10000, 10) Check: Withdrawal reporting limit depends on the jurisdiction. Verify. Tag: Structuring · AML · Account · 7d
    9.1-09 Cash withdrawals repeatedly at a daily ATM maximum Hitting ATM limit on consecutive days. Abstraction Rule AtmMaxDays7d (Search Key CardId, Search Interval Days / 7, Function Type Distinct Count on BusinessDate, a date-only placeholder field) over withdrawals whose amount is within a band of the daily maximum held in Dictionary AtmDailyMaximum. A distinct-day count equal to the window length means every day in the window hit the maximum, and the alerting rule reads Abstraction.AtmMaxDays7d.MatchGreaterOrEqual(7). Matched = Payload.TxnType.RequireEqual("CashWithdrawal").Against(Payload.CurrencyAmount).MatchRatioInRange(Dictionary.AtmDailyMaximum, 0.999, 1) Check: ATM daily maximums vary by issuer and operator. Verify the Dictionary value. Tag: Structuring · Both · Card · 7d
